Steward: Family Assistant for iPhone

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Steward is a family assistant for iPhone. This policy says what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version

Steward's maker collects nothing. There is no Steward account, no Steward server, no analytics and no advertising. What you put into Steward stays on your iPhone unless you choose to share it.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your lists, items, notes and settings.
  • What you say. Speech is turned into text on your device, and your notes are read by Apple's on-device intelligence.
  • Photos of schedules you scan. They are read on your device and not kept by Steward.
  • The stores and places you attach to a list.

When information leaves your iPhone

Only in these cases, and only because you asked:

  • Calendar and reminders. Events and reminders you create are saved to the calendar and reminders on your iPhone. If you sync those with iCloud or another service, that service's policy applies.
  • Family Sharing (optional). If you turn it on, your lists are kept in your own iCloud account and shared only with the people you invite. Apple stores them; Steward's maker cannot see them. Turn it off at any time in Settings.
  • Google Calendar (optional). If you are signed in to Google in Settings, the events you create are sent directly from your iPhone to Google, and invitations go to the guests you name. Steward's maker never sees them. You can sign out at any time in Settings.
  • Finding stores and places. When you search for a store or place, the search and your approximate location go to Apple Maps to find results.
  • Store icons. For a list named after a store, Steward asks Apple's App Store for that store's app icon. Only the store's name is sent.
  • Contacts. When you ask Steward to invite someone, it looks up their email in your contacts on your device. The address is used only for that invitation.

Purchases

Tips and the Steward Supporter subscription are sold through Apple's in-app purchase. Apple handles the payment; Steward's maker never sees your payment details.

Permissions

Steward asks for the microphone and speech recognition (to add things by voice), calendar and reminders (to save what you ask for), location (to show the right list at a store), contacts (to invite people), the camera (to scan schedules) and notifications (to show a list). Each is optional and can be changed in the Settings app.

Children

Steward is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

Deleting your information

Delete the app and everything Steward stored is removed from your iPhone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
