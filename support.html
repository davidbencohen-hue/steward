Common questions

How do I add something? Tap Talk and say it, or tap the + beside it to type. "Diapers from Target and soccer Saturday at 9" becomes a list item and a calendar event.

How do I share my lists with my spouse? Open Settings, then Family Sharing, and tap Invite My Family. You both need Steward and to be signed in to iCloud.

My list does not appear when I open a store's app. Open the list and tap Test It Now. If it did not run, open the Shortcuts app, open the Steward shortcut and switch its automation on. This needs iOS 27.

How do I add a school calendar? Tap the + beside Talk and choose a photo, screenshots, a PDF or a link under Scan a schedule. Tick the events you want.

How do I cancel Steward Supporter? Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Steward.

How do I restore a purchase? Open the Tip Jar in the app and tap Restore Purchases.
